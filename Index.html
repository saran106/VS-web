FAQ

Your questions, our answers

Got any queries about our tailoring services? Here's what customers ask us most.

What types of outfits do you stitch?+
We stitch blouses, lehengas, gowns, salwars and more — customised to your style and fit.
Can I bring my own fabric?+
Yes — bring your own fabric or choose from our curated collection.
How long does an order take?+
Most pieces take 7–14 days depending on the embroidery and detailing involved.
Do you offer custom embroidery?+
Absolutely — aari, zardozi and machine embroidery are all available on request.
Do you offer alterations?+
Yes, we offer free alterations within 7 days of delivery if the fit isn't perfect.
